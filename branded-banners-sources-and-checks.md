24 opaque PNGs, each exactly 2400 × 650 px, with an embedded sRGB profile. The first 10 previously delivered banners were left unchanged.

These are AI-composed product scenes based on official catalogue photographs of real products. They retain visible product branding. They are not unaltered original product photographs; small packaging text and some product details can differ in the generated composition. Apparel branding was not invented where it was absent from the source photo.

## Checks

For every file: left 432 columns exactly match the specified background; all four outer edges match; top and bottom 52 pixels and right 96 pixels stay clear; products fit within the right-side zone and remain complete in a right-half crop; headline and all four category labels use the exact workbook text. Brand names, real product labels and actual garment graphics are permitted under the revised brief. No live people, models, hands, prices, discount badges or separate promotional copy were added. Portrait artwork already printed on the Hyuga ONE pouch is part of the actual packaging.
